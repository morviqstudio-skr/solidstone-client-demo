What we build

Every surface starts with the base underneath it.

Solidstone handles the full scope of hardscaping — from the first excavation to the final sanding and sealing — so each piece is built to the same standard.

Interlocking driveways

Herringbone, running-bond and custom paver patterns designed for GTA freeze-thaw cycles.

Walkways & patios

Interlocking and natural stone walkways, patios and pool decks shaped around how you use the yard.

Retaining walls

Engineered stone retaining walls that manage grade and drainage, not just hold back soil.

Brick masonry & repointing

Chimney rebuilds, tuckpointing and lifting or relaying old brick that's shifted or worn.

Pool deck interlocking

Lift-and-relay and new-build pool surrounds in slip-resistant natural stone and pavers.

Drainage & waterproofing

Grading and drainage work that keeps water moving away from foundations and low spots.

Landscaping & gardening

Planting beds, curbing and softscape design that ties the hardscape into the rest of the property.

Concrete driveways

Poured and stamped concrete for driveways and aprons where that's the right fit.
